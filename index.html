About

Hi, I'm Chandler.

I'm a website and app designer who recently moved to Oklahoma City. I started reaching out to local shops after noticing how many great businesses, with hundreds of five-star reviews, had no website at all or one that didn't work on a phone.

So I build the site first and show you. If you love it, it's yours for one flat price. If not, no hard feelings. I'm local, I answer my phone, and I'm happy to make changes.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. I build a preview using your business info and send you the link. You pay nothing unless you decide you want it live.

How long does it take?

Most previews are ready within a day or two. Once you approve, your site can go live the same day.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and if you ever want to move on, I'll hand you the files.

Are there monthly fees?

No. Hosting is included in the one-time price. The $15/month care plan is optional, for people who want me to handle updates.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Absolutely. I can redesign it, keep all your content, fix what's broken and make it work great on phones.

How do I pay?

Cash App, Venmo, PayPal or cash, after you've approved the final site.
