About us

KMK Auto Repair & Body Shop is a locally owned, full-service shop serving Oklahoma City drivers. From routine maintenance to collision and hail damage, we handle domestic and import cars, trucks and SUVs. Open evenings on weekdays, so you can drop off after work.
