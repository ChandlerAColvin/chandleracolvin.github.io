our story

Every loaf starts the night before. Our sourdough ferments for 48 hours, our croissants are folded by hand over three days, and our ovens fire up at 4 AM so the first bake is cooling when you walk in.

We've been on Maple Street since 1998, using Oklahoma-milled flour, local eggs and real butter. No shortcuts, no mixes. Just good bread, made the slow way.
